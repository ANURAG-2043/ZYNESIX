About Zynexis

Engineering capability. Indigenous ambition.

A young aerospace technology company focused on developing UAV electronics and integrated systems in India.

Our story

Focused on the systems that make flight possible.

Founded in 2026, Zynexis Technologies is building an indigenous product and engineering portfolio for the evolving UAV and aerospace ecosystem. Our first and only current product is the Power Distribution Unit. Flight-control electronics, sensing, battery technologies and other systems form our future product ambitions.

We aim to work closely with platform developers, OEMs and technical partners — from early requirements and prototyping through integration and production.
